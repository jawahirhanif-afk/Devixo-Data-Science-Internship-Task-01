# Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Supermarket Sales

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the Data Science Internship – Task 01 at Devixo Solutions.

The objective of this project is to perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a supermarket sales dataset to understand sales patterns, customer behavior, product performance, and business trends.

## Dataset Information
- **Dataset Name:** Supermarket Sales
- **Source:** [Kaggle – Supermarket Sales](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aungpyaeap/supermarket-sales)
- **Records:** 1,000
- **Columns:** 17
- **File Format:** CSV

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Google Colab
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn

## Project Tasks
1. Data Import and Understanding
2. Data Cleaning and Preprocessing
3. Numerical and Categorical Feature Analysis
4. Correlation Analysis
5. Data Visualization
6. Business Insights
7. Business Recommendations

## Data Cleaning
- Checked missing values.
- Checked duplicate records.
- Reviewed data types.
- Converted the Date column into datetime format.
- Converted the Time column into time format.
- Examined outliers and retained valid high-value transactions.

## Visualizations
A total of 13 visualizations were prepared, including:
- Numerical feature histograms
- Total sales boxplot
- Correlation heatmap
- Sales by product line
- Sales by branch
- Payment method distribution
- Average rating by product line
- Daily sales trend
- Sales by customer type
- Transactions by hour
- Quantity versus total sales scatter plot

## Key Findings
- Food and Beverages generated the highest total sales among product lines.
- Branch C recorded the highest total sales.
- Members generated slightly higher sales than normal customers.
- Female customers contributed higher total sales than male customers.
- Cash recorded the highest sales by payment method.
- E-wallet was the most frequently used payment method.
- Home and Lifestyle had the highest average transaction value.
- The average customer rating was approximately 6.97.

## Business Recommendations
- Investigate opportunities to improve Health and Beauty sales.
- Study Branch C's performance and identify practices that may benefit other branches.
- Strengthen customer loyalty and membership programs.
- Support different customer payment preferences.
- Investigate customer feedback for Home and Lifestyle products.
